About Hack-Z 2026

Hack-Z 2026 is the flagship intra-college hackathon organized by V Technocrats — the student tech forum of the BCA Department, VIMTECH Tumkur.

This year marks an exciting milestone — a full 6-hour innovation sprint where the brightest student minds come together to ideate, build, and present solutions that matter. Six focused domains. Real problems. Real impact.

This is where innovation meets execution. Hack-Z 2026 is built for builders, problem solvers, and future technologists who want to push boundaries and create something the campus has never seen before.

How You'll Be Judged

Three rigorous phases. One winner. Zero shortcuts.

 Phase 1 · 10:00 AM
🕙
01
Idea Screening
Teams present a short PPT to mentors. Evaluated on clarity of problem, originality of solution, and feasibility within the 6-hour sprint.
  • Problem Statement — Is it well-defined and relevant?
  • Proposed Solution — Is the approach creative?
  • Implementation Plan — Realistic for 6 hours?
  • Innovation Score — Does it go beyond obvious?
 Phase 2 · 2:00 PM
🕑
02
Mid Evaluation
Live progress check mid-sprint. Mentors review development status and provide targeted feedback to help teams refine before the final round.
  • Development Progress — How much is built?

// Rules

Hack-Z 2026 Rule Book

V Technocrats · BCA Department · VIMTECH Tumkur

📌 General Guidelines

  • This is an intra-college hackathon exclusively for VIMTECH students.
  • All participants must register through the official platform.
  • Each participant can be part of only one team.
  • Teams must adhere to ethical coding standards throughout.

👥 Team Formation

  • Team size: 2 to 3 members only.
  • One member must act as Team Leader.
  • Team changes are not allowed after registration closes.
  • All members must be enrolled at VIMTECH.

💻 Development Rules

  • All projects must be original — built during the event.
  • Use of open-source libraries is permitted and encouraged.
  • Pre-built templates must be declared during submission.
  • Plagiarism results in immediate disqualification.

📤 Submission Guidelines

  • Submit via the official dashboard before the deadline.
  • Include: Source Code (GitHub link)
  • Include: Presentation (PPT format — required)
  • No demo video required. Live demo at finals.
  • Late submissions will not be accepted.

🏆 Judging Criteria

  • Innovation & Creativity 30%
  • Technical Complexity 25%
  • UI/UX Design & Usability 20%
  • Real-world Impact 15%
  • Presentation Quality 10%

⚠️ Code of Conduct

  • Maintain professional behavior at all times.
  • No harassment or misconduct of any form.
  • Follow all instructions from organizers and judges.
  • Violations lead to immediate disqualification.

📢 Final Notes

  • All decisions by the judging panel are final and binding.
  • Organizers reserve the right to modify rules at their discretion.
  • Meals, snacks, and high-speed Wi-Fi will be provided throughout.
  • Have fun, learn hard, and innovate boldly! 🚀
